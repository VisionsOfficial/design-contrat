Kuzzle sent the DPA back with two edits. What changed, and can I accept it as it stands?

Two clauses moved between version 3 and version 4.

6.2 — Sub-processor notification Notice cut from 30 days to 10 business days, and your right to object became a right to “raise reasonable concerns”.
9.4 — Audit On-site audits capped at one per calendar year, 20 days' notice.

I would not accept 6.2 as written: without a right to object you cannot block a sub-processor you consider non-compliant.

Legal · 09:13 · read version 3 and 4
What is our exposure if we accept it anyway?

You keep responsibility for the processing chain while losing the means to control it:

  • A sub-processor can be added 10 days after notice, including outside the EEA.
  • Your Article 28 documentation stops matching the signed contract.
  • Fleet telemetry and Traffic anomaly detection inherit the weaker terms.

Counter-proposal ready: keep the 10-day notice, restore the right to object, suspend transfers while an objection is open.
